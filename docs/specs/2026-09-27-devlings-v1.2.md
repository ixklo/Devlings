# Devlings v1.2: calmer, and a hello

Status: shipped in 1.2.0 · Builds on `2026-09-27-devlings-v1.1.md` (and through it the v1.0 contract). These changes come from a research review of what makes people keep a desktop companion (the owner approved all three) and follow the standing rules in v1.1 §2a.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D23.1 | **Long states settle.** A looping row can carry a `RestPolicy` (`src/sprite/petAnimation.ts`): after `afterMs` of playing back to back it finishes the play in progress, then holds frame 0 for `minMs`–`maxMs` between plays (`useSpriteClip`, `spriteClock.restLength`). Idle rests from the start (4–8 s, as before). Working settles after 60 s (1.5–3 s rests), failed after 8 s (6–10 s), review after 20 s (4–8 s). Waiting and setup never rest; the drag run and one-shots are unchanged; reduced motion still shows a still frame. | Motion in peripheral vision captures attention automatically, so a state that lasts should move less once it has been seen. "Needs you" is the one state meant to keep catching the eye, and the change *into* a new state stays noticeable. |
| D23.2 | **Say when the system won't show notifications.** Windows: `notify::system_allows` reads `ToastNotifier::Setting` for Devlings' app id (the `windows` crate, the same 0.61 tauri-winrt-notification already uses). `AppState::system_notifications_off` is refreshed at launch (through `recheck_setup`), on a setup recheck, when Settings opens and when the Notifications switch goes on, and reaches the page as `setup.systemNotificationsOff`. Settings' Notifications row then reads "Windows has notifications turned off, so none will appear. Turn them on in Windows Settings → System → Notifications.", and the bell's tooltip adds "but Windows has them turned off". Other platforms report nothing. | Found in v1.1 QA: with Windows notifications off for all apps, the switch said on while every toast was dropped silently. |
| D23.3 | **Welcome back.** `presence::Presence` samples the time since the last input (Windows `GetLastInputInfo`) on the backend's 1 s tick: 10 minutes or more with no input counts as away (a locked screen too), and the first reading of fresh input (≤ 2 s) after that sends `pet-welcome` to the pet window once. The page plays the hover wave for it, so the wave cooldown, drag and jump rules and reduced motion all apply. No reading off Windows, so no wave there. | Coming back gets a small greeting and leaving costs nothing: the positive half of "never punish absence" (v1.1 §2a). |
| D23.4 | **A shown pet can't get lost.** The pet window is not minimizable (`tauri.conf.json`), a pet that gets minimized anyway is put back 400 ms later (`shell::track_minimized`), `show_pet` puts it back too, and the backend's tick checks every 3 s (`overlay::RESCUE_CHECK_MS`) that a shown pet's window is neither minimized nor on no monitor at all (`overlay::pet_is_lost`); `overlay::rescue_pet` then un-minimizes it, shows its page (design D17 had hidden it) and re-places the sprite at its saved spot (with D9's fallback). A hidden pet (tray, Ctrl+Alt+P, hide for an hour) is left alone. Every rescue runs on the main thread (`overlay::rescue_pet_soon`; X11 aborts on window calls from another thread) and only on Windows, where a minimized pet has nowhere to come back from and window positions can be trusted (X11 can report a spot before the window manager places the window; Wayland reports none). The periodic check waits 10 s after a rescue before it may start another, so a wrongly reported position can't loop. Shipped in 1.2.2. | Reported by the owner ("pet disappears sometimes"); reproduced: once minimized, the pet has no taskbar button, and showing it again left the window parked near -32000, -32000 with its page hidden until a restart |
| D23.5 | **The control bar only shows on hover or use.** `barVisible` is `hover || view.kind !== "bubbles"`: the pointer on the pet or the bar (the backend's `pet-pointer`, with `HOVER_GRACE_MS` after it leaves), keyboard focus in the dock, or a card open (composer, mini chat). It no longer stays up while threads exist or something is hidden (v1.1 D22.7), so the hidden-threads count on the Show threads button is seen on hover; the pet's own mood (waiting, failed) still says when something needs you. Shipped in 1.2.3. | The owner: "Taskbar should be hidden until you hover devling or are actively interacting with it" |
